About

Earlier this summer, CMI began piloting career-related events for our cohort under the banner "CMI Connect." Following a successful alumni networking pilot in late summer, CMI Connect was folded into Medill Career Services as part of their new fall programming, which is, by pure coincidence, called aptly IMC Connects.

Career Services will continue to host events on campus this fall. This portion of IMC Connects focuses on 1:1 coffee chats and will remain student-run. On this website, you will find 100+ Northwestern alumni from across the world who are eager to support your growth. You can submit up to 3 alumni for coffee-chat introductions. Please feel free to use this resource as you job search this quarter.

As with last time, IMC Connects programming is open to all members of the IMC cohort.

If you have any questions, please feel free to message me (Gaby) via WhatsApp or text.

How does this work?

Browse the directory, add alumni to your list, submit up to 3 and wait for an email introduction to each of them. Once you're connected, it's up to you to lead the conversation.

Note: Since alumni have indicated their availability, the site runs on a first-come, first-serve basis. If an alum's slots fill up before you've submitted, you will not be able to request them.

Are matches guaranteed?
For the up to 3 alumni you submit, yes, as long as they still have an open spot when you submit. For your wish list, it depends on whether the alums are still available after the deadline.
Can I pick fewer than 3?
Absolutely. 3 is the maximum, not a requirement.
Can I just pick randomly?
To respect alumni's time and be fair to other students, please do not request a coffee chat with someone if you don't plan to follow through.
What happens after we're connected via email?
My introductory email will ask the alumni to share their availability with you. If you haven't heard back after 1 day, shoot them a follow-up message and please move me to BCC.
Are these 1:1 coffee chats or group sessions?
1:1 only! We want these chats to be as free and helpful for your individual needs as possible.
If these are 1:1, why are there other students copied on our email?
To avoid spamming the alums with multiple emails.
What if none of the alumni I want are available?
The availability you see on this site was given to us by the alums themselves, so we can't overcommit them to more coffee chats than they can handle. However, if your preferred alums are full, you're welcome to try connecting with them via LinkedIn.
Is this affiliated with Career Services?
Yes and no. This initiative started through CMI and has been folded into Career Services. However, it is still student-run. Please feel free to reach out to Career Services for more targeted career advising!
I'm nervous about coffee chatting. What can I do?
Prof. Jacqueline Babb has very kindly offered to host mock 1:1 coffee chats to anyone that feels uneasy about networking. Please shoot her an email and she'll be happy to help!
I already submitted. How do I change my pins?
Use the "Edit your response" link from the confirmation email you got when you first submitted. This site only accepts 1 response so if you try resubmitting here, your submission will get blocked as a duplicate.
